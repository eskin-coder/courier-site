Courier

A fast, native Gmail client for macOS.

Courier is a personal mail app for the Mac. It shows all your Gmail accounts in one inbox, groups letters by sender, copies one-time codes in a click and can be driven entirely from the keyboard.

How it works

  • You sign in with your Google account. Courier talks directly to the Gmail API from your Mac.
  • There is no Courier server. Your mail, contacts and sign-in tokens never pass through any third party.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac.
