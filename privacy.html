Privacy Policy

Courier for macOS · Last updated October 13, 2026

Courier is a mail app that runs entirely on your Mac. This page explains what data it accesses and what it does with it.

Data Courier accesses

When you sign in with Google, you grant Courier access to:

  • Your Gmail messages, labels and settings, to show, send, organize and delete mail at your request.
  • Your contacts and the people you have emailed (read-only), to suggest addresses when you write a letter.
  • Your name, email address and profile picture, to show which account is which.

How the data is used and stored

  • All data is downloaded directly from Google to your Mac and used only to provide the app's features to you.
  • Courier has no servers. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain. Cached mail stays on your Mac.
  • Courier does not use your data for advertising, does not sell it and does not use it to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Courier's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Remove an account in Courier's Settings to delete its tokens and cached mail from your Mac. You can also revoke Courier's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on Courier's Google sign-in screen.
